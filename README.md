# Bible & Quran Study

An installable PWA for comparative Bible–Quran study and apologetics: Search & Reveal across the full Quran (Yusuf Ali 1934 and Pickthall 1930) and the KJV Bible, a catalog of cited weak points and controversies (Quran, Muhammad's life, hadith, sira, tafsir, fiqh), each with Muslim responses and Biblical contrasts, a guide to the Other Books of Islam, side-by-side comparison, notes, bookmarks, quiz and flashcards, a printable study sheet, and private Gist sync.

Texts: Quran translations from api.alquran.cloud (`en.yusufali`, `en.pickthall`); KJV from thiagobodruk/bible (public domain). Hadith quotations come from sunnah.com, and each one is linked on its card.
